Celebrated Indian Authors

Rabindranath Tagore

Notable Works: Gitanjali, The Home and the World

Nobel Laureate, poet, novelist, and playwright; known for his profound literary contributions.

R.K. Narayan

Notable Works: Malgudi Days, Swami and Friends

Famous for his simple yet profound storytelling that captures the essence of Indian life.

Salman Rushdie

Notable Works: Midnight’s Children, The Satanic Verses

Notable for blending magical realism with historical themes in his narratives.

Arundhati Roy

Notable Works: The God of Small Things

Won the Booker Prize; known for her activism and socio-political essays.

Vikram Seth

Notable Works: A Suitable Boy

Acclaimed for his sprawling tale of post-independence India.

Ruskin Bond

Notable Works: The Blue Umbrella, Our Trees Still Grow in Dehra

A beloved author of short stories and novels, capturing the beauty of nature.

Khushwant Singh

Notable Works: Train to Pakistan

Renowned for his powerful narrative on the Partition of India.

Amitav Ghosh

Notable Works: The Ibis Trilogy, The Shadow Lines

Known for focusing on colonial history and migration in his works.

Chetan Bhagat

Notable Works: Five Point Someone, 2 States

A modern writer appealing to young readers with relatable themes.

Shashi Tharoor

Notable Works: An Era of Darkness, The Great Indian Novel

A historian and politician known for his insightful analysis of India's history and politics.
