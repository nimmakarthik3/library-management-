Best Indian Books Across Different Genres

Classics & Literary Fiction

Midnight’s Children

by Salman Rushdie

A magical realist take on India’s independence and partition.

The God of Small Things

by Arundhati Roy

A poignant story of love, caste, and family in Kerala.

Train to Pakistan

by Khushwant Singh

A powerful novel on the horrors of Partition.

Modern & Contemporary Fiction

The White Tiger

by Aravind Adiga

A dark, witty critique of India’s class divide.

The Palace of Illusions

by Chitra Banerjee Divakaruni

A retelling of the Mahabharata from Draupadi’s perspective.

Historical & Mythological Fiction

Jaya: An Illustrated Retelling of the Mahabharata

by Devdutt Pattanaik

A beautifully narrated and visualized version of the epic.

A Suitable Boy

by Vikram Seth

A sweeping tale of love, politics, and family in post-independence India.

Non-Fiction & Memoirs

India After Gandhi

by Ramachandra Guha

An in-depth history of independent India.

An Era of Darkness

by Shashi Tharoor

A critical look at British colonial rule in India.

Autobiography of a Yogi

by Paramahansa Yogananda

A spiritual classic that influenced many global thinkers.
